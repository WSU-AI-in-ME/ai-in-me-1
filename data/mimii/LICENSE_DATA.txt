These audio files are a derived subset of:

MIMII Dataset: Sound Dataset for Malfunctioning Industrial Machine
Investigation and Inspection, public 1.0.

Original authors:
Harsh Purohit, Ryo Tanabe, Kenji Ichige, Takashi Endo, Yuki Nikaido,
Kaori Suefusa, and Yohei Kawaguchi (Hitachi, Ltd.).

Original dataset record:
https://zenodo.org/records/3384388
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3384388

The original Zenodo record states that MIMII public 1.0 is distributed under
the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license
(CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made for this teaching subset:
- selected a deterministic subset of 0 dB recordings;
- retained microphone Channel 1 only (source channel index 0);
- converted 8-channel WAV files to mono 16-bit PCM WAV files without amplitude
  normalization, cropping, slicing, resampling, or compression;
- reorganized files and added course metadata; and
- normalized the source condition label "abnormal" to "anomaly".
